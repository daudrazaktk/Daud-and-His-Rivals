🎮 Steamworks PC Packaging & Online Server Roadmap
1. How Friends Play Together Right Now (Web & Cloud Rooms):

This build includes a live Cloud Multiplayer Sync engine (`🌐 ONLINE MULTIPLAYER SERVERS`) plus cross-tab LAN broadcast. When you and your friends enter the same Room Code (e.g., PK-OFFICIAL-1), your 3D positions, rotations, team uniforms, and gunshots sync live on the map and radar.

2. Wrapping Into a Native Windows `.EXE` for Steam (`Electron`):

To upload Daud And His Rivals to Steamworks as a native PC game:

mkdir daud-rivals-steam && cd daud-rivals-steam
npm init -y
npm install electron electron-builder steamworks.js --save-dev
# Place index.html in the folder, create main.js loading index.html in fullscreen, then build:
npx electron-builder --win --x64
3. Steamworks SDK & Steam Overlay / Lobby Integration:

Register on partner.steamgames.com (Steam Direct), get your `AppID`, and place `steam_appid.txt` next to your executable. Using `steamworks.js`, your friends can click "Invite to Game" directly in the Steam Friends List.
